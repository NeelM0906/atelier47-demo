East River Emerald Cut

For Priyanka, who wanted it to read sideways

A lab-grown emerald cut set east to west on a slim 18k yellow gold band.

Priyanka wanted something that looked like jewelry first and an engagement ring second. Setting the stone horizontally made it feel like an architectural bar. We chose a lab-grown stone so the budget could go into a heavier band.
