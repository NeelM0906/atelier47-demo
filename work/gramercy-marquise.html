Gramercy Marquise

For Elena, who loves old things

A north-south marquise with milgrain edges and a hand-engraved gallery.

Elena collects Victorian lockets. We drew the gallery from one of them, then Elias hand-engraved the shoulders after casting. It took two extra weeks and was worth every day.
