The Montauk Oval

For Dana and Theo, a proposal at Ditch Plains

A 2.1 carat oval on a knife-edge band with a hidden halo under the stone.

Theo came in with one photo and one rule: nothing that catches on a wetsuit. We kept the profile low, used four claw prongs, and tucked a hidden halo of twelve diamonds under the stone so it only shows from the side.
