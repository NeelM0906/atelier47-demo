Fifth Avenue Cushion

For Rachel, after her grandmother's ring

A cushion cut in a fine micro-pavé halo, sized to echo a 1950s family ring.

Rachel brought her grandmother's ring for reference. We measured its proportions, then rebuilt the idea in platinum with a halo that sits flush with the stone instead of standing proud of it.
