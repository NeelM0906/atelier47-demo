About

Atelier47 is five people and six benches.

Founded in 2009 by Ilana Roth in a single room on West 47th Street. Today the atelier designs and makes about two hundred pieces a year, each for one person, with no showroom stock and no commission sales staff.

  • Ilana Roth

    Founder and lead designer

    Graduate Gemologist (placeholder credential) · 17 years

    Ilana trained at a Rhode Island casting house before opening the atelier in 2009. She sits in on every first consultation and draws the first sketch by hand while you talk.

  • Teodor Vasquez

    Master setter

    Bench since 1998 · 28 years

    Teodor sets every center stone under the microscope and checks each prong twice before it leaves his bench. He likes clients to see their stone go in.

  • Sun-hee Park

    CAD designer

    Matrix and Rhino · 11 years

    Sun-hee turns the sketch into a rotating 3D model you can approve on your phone. She will adjust a gallery rail by a tenth of a millimeter if you ask.

  • Elias Mendel

    Bench jeweler and polisher

    Third generation on 47th Street · 22 years

    Elias cleans up each casting, fits the band, and gives the final polish. He also handles resizing and repairs for anything we have made.

  • Priya Raman

    Client advisor

    Stone sourcing · 8 years

    Priya is your single point of contact. She sources the stone shortlist, sends every update, and answers WhatsApp messages the same day.

How we work

Three promises we keep.

You meet the maker. The person who sets your stone is in the room at your consultation.

Prices in writing. You get an itemized quote for the stone, the setting and the labor before you pay a deposit.

No pressure, ever. We will never run a countdown on you. Take the brief home and decide when you are ready.

Consultations

Sit down with the people who will make it.

60 minutes in the atelier or 45 minutes on video. $250 retainer, credited in full to your piece.
