Demo disclosure

This website is a design template. Atelier47, its address, phone numbers, team, commissions, prices, reviews and credentials are fictional placeholders. Jewelry images are computer illustrations, not photographs of real pieces. Grading report numbers are intentionally withheld and the sample report is not a real document.

Privacy

We collect only what you send us through our forms: your name, contact details and what you tell us about the piece you want. We use it to answer you and to make your commission. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

This site uses no cookies and no third-party trackers. Your saved pieces and a copy of your design brief are kept in your own browser's local storage so they are there when you come back. Clear your browser data to remove them.

To see or delete what we hold about you, email studio@atelier47.example.

Terms

Illustrative price ranges on this site are estimates, not offers. Your price is confirmed in a written quote after your consultation. Custom pieces are made to order and are not returnable once casting begins, except for manufacturing defects, which our lifetime warranty covers.

The design retainer is credited in full toward your commission. It is refundable if you cancel more than 48 hours before your consultation or if we cannot make the piece you want.

Accessibility

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. The site works with a keyboard and screen readers, respects your reduced motion setting and uses no popups or timers. If anything gets in your way, call +1 212-555-0147 and we will help directly.
