The process

Five steps. About six weeks.

You approve every stage before we move to the next. Nothing is cast until you have turned the 3D model in your hand.

  1. 01

    Consult

    Week 0

    An hour at the atelier or on video with a designer and the bench jeweler who will make your piece.

  2. 02

    Sketch and stones

    Week 1

    Hand sketches and a shortlist of three to five certified stones, side by side.

  3. 03

    CAD

    Weeks 2 to 3

    A precise 3D model you can rotate on your phone. Two rounds of changes are included.

  4. 04

    At the bench

    Weeks 4 to 5

    Cast in your chosen metal, then finished, set and polished in our suite on 47th Street.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Week 6

    Collect it in person, or receive it insured and signature-required anywhere in the US.

Craft and materials

Everything but the casting happens at our benches.

Six benches, one microscope setting station and a laser welder, twelve floors above the street. Casting is done by a partner casting house in the same building, and every piece comes back to us for finishing, setting and polish.

Platinum 950
Naturally white, dense and hypoallergenic. Our default for diamond heads.
18k yellow gold
Warm, rich color. 75 percent pure gold, alloyed for strength.
18k rose gold
Copper in the alloy gives the blush. Softer on fair and olive skin.
18k white gold
Lighter than platinum and rhodium finished. Recycled gold on request.
