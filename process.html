The process

From first sketch to your hand in six weeks.

Every commission follows the same path. You approve each stage before the next begins, and you always know who is working on your piece.

  1. 01

    Consult · Week 0, 60 minutes

    Consultation

    We talk about the person, the hand, the budget and the date you are working toward. You try on sample settings in every shape and metal. Ilana sketches while you talk. You leave with a written brief and a budget split between stone and setting.

  2. 02

    Sketch and stones · Week 1, 5 to 7 days

    Sketch and stone shortlist

    Priya sources three to five stones that fit the brief and sends each grading report with a video under daylight and under our lamp. You can view them in person before you choose. Two or three hand sketches arrive in the same message.

  3. 03

    CAD · Weeks 2 to 3

    CAD render

    Sun-hee builds the ring to the millimeter around your actual stone. You get photoreal renders and a rotating model. Two rounds of revisions are included. A 50 percent deposit at CAD approval starts production.

  4. 04

    At the bench · Week 4

    Casting

    Your approved model is printed in wax and cast at our partner casting house two floors down. Elias cleans and files the raw casting, fits the band to your size and pre-polishes it.

  5. 05

    At the bench · Week 5

    Setting

    Teodor sets the center stone under the microscope, checks every prong and adds any pavé or halo stones. You are welcome to watch your stone go in. Final polish and a quality check follow.

  6. 06

    Delivery · Week 6

    Delivery

    Collect in the atelier, or we ship fully insured with signature required. You receive the grading report, an independent-format appraisal for insurance, a care guide and free cleaning and prong checks for life.

Payments

Three payments, each tied to something you approve.

  1. At booking

    $250

    Design retainer, credited in full. Refunded if we cannot make what you want.

  2. At CAD approval

    50%

    Deposit once you approve the 3D model and your stone. Production starts here.

  3. At delivery

    Balance

    Paid when you have seen the finished piece. Card, wire or Zelle.

Good to know

Timing, changes and rush orders.

Changes are free until you approve the CAD model. Two rounds of CAD revisions are included.

Rush timelines of three to four weeks are possible depending on the bench schedule. Ask at the consultation.

Proposal secrecy is part of the job. We can send every update to one person only, and we hold finished rings in our safe until the day.
