Anatomy and cut

Cut is about where the light goes.

A well cut diamond takes light in through the table and sends it back out to your eye. Too shallow and light leaks out of the bottom. Too deep and it escapes through the sides. Both look darker and smaller than they should.

Grading reports give a cut grade for round brilliants only. For fancy shapes we judge proportions by eye and by the numbers, and we show you both.

Origin

Lab-grown and natural, honestly compared.

Both are real diamonds with the same chemistry and hardness. The difference is origin, price and how they hold value. We sell both and have no preference for which you choose.

Certification

A grading report is the stone's passport.

Every center diamond we sell above 0.30 carats comes with a report from an independent laboratory. We do not grade our own center stones for sale.

GIA
Gemological Institute of America. The nonprofit that created the 4Cs grading system. Our first choice for natural diamonds.
IGI
International Gemological Institute. The most common laboratory for lab-grown diamonds, with clear and consistent reports.

Each report has a unique number, usually laser inscribed on the girdle. You can look it up on the laboratory's own website, and we show you the inscription under magnification before you buy.

Responsible sourcing

What conflict-free means, and what it does not.

We buy diamonds only from suppliers who comply with the Kimberley Process, the international scheme that blocks rough diamonds that fund rebel movements, and who pass a written warranty to us under the World Diamond Council System of Warranties.

The Kimberley Process defines conflict narrowly, so we go further where we can. We ask for country of origin, offer stones with traceable origin on request, and offer recycled platinum and gold for every design.

Lab-grown diamonds avoid mining altogether. Their footprint depends on the energy used to grow them, and we share what our growers disclose.
