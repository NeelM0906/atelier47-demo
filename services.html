Services

Care, resizing, appraisals and repair.

For pieces we made and pieces we did not. Prices are listed so you know what to expect before you walk in.

Cleaning and inspection

Ultrasonic and steam cleaning, a prong check under the microscope and a polish of the band. We recommend it every six months for a ring you wear daily.

Price
Complimentary for our pieces. $35 for others.
Turnaround
While you wait, about 20 minutes

Resizing

Up or down by up to two sizes on most rings. Eternity and some pavé bands cannot be resized, and we will say so before we start. First resize free within a year on pieces we made.

Price
From $85 in gold, $150 in platinum
Turnaround
3 to 5 business days

Repair and restoration

Retipping, rebuilding worn shanks, replacing lost melee, re-rhodium plating for white gold and restoring engraving. We quote in writing before any work begins.

Price
Prong retipping from $60 per prong. Quoted after inspection.
Turnaround
1 to 3 weeks

Insurance appraisal

A written appraisal for insurance replacement value with measurements, photographs and stone grades. Prepared by our gemologist in house. We price it per piece, never as a percentage of value.

Price
$150 for the first piece, $75 each additional
Turnaround
2 business days, or same day by appointment

Heirloom redesign

Reuse a family stone or metal in a new design. We test and grade your stones first and tell you honestly what can be reused safely.

Price
Book a consultation. Retainer credited to the work.
Turnaround
5 to 8 weeks

Insurance

Insure it the week you get it.

Most homeowners and renters policies cap jewelry cover at a low amount. Ask your insurer for a scheduled rider, or use a specialist jewelry insurer. Either one will ask for an appraisal.

We include a written appraisal with every commission. For other pieces, our appraisals list measurements, photographs and stone grades. Update them every three to five years as metal and stone prices move.
